## What I'm working on

### Byte-Sized Python

[Byte-Sized Python](https://bytesizedpython.org) is a 501(c)(3) I started in summer 2025. Paid coding classes cost money a lot of students don't have, and many free options assume a recent computer. I'm the founder, president, and tech lead.

- We've taught more than 200 students in Charlotte and internationally.
- Koda, our AI tutor built for lower-end hardware, has supported more than 330.
- I designed and taught a five-day summer 2025 course with 40+ exercises to 70+ students.
- I build the software: [Koda Studio](https://studio.bytesizedpython.org), a browser-based Python editor with the tutor built in, and [Koda Live](https://live.bytesizedpython.org), where a teacher starts a session and students join with a six-character code. There's also an internal stats dashboard and a starter kit for people who want to launch their own chapter.

### YETI Robotics

On [FRC Team 3506](https://yetirobotics.org) I lead a 30-person programming and electrical team and write the Java (WPILib) for every subsystem on the robot.

- A three-camera AprilTag vision pipeline, fused with drivetrain odometry through a Kalman filter.
- Auto-alignment, plus an interpolated lookup map from shot distance to flywheel speed.
- PID tuning for a swerve drive on Kraken X60 motors, and current limits to keep the robot out of brownout.
- Our autonomous routines added about 50 points a match.
