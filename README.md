## 🌱 What I'm working on

### [Byte-Sized Python](https://bytesizedpython.org) · 501(c)(3) nonprofit
Teaches Python to students who can't afford paid classes. I'm the founder, president, and tech lead.

- **200+** students taught in Charlotte and internationally
- **330+** students supported by **Koda**, our AI tutor built for lower-end hardware
- Designed and taught a five-day summer 2025 course (40+ exercises, 70+ students)
- Built the tools behind it: [Koda Studio](https://studio.bytesizedpython.org) (browser-based Python IDE with the AI tutor), [Koda Live](https://live.bytesizedpython.org) (classroom sessions with a six-character join code), BSP Stats, and a Chapter Starter Kit for people launching their own peer-led chapters

### [YETI Robotics](https://yetirobotics.org) · FRC Team 3506
Controls manager. I write Java (WPILib) for every subsystem on the robot, plus vision, auto-alignment, and localization.

- Three-camera AprilTag pipeline fused with odometry through a Kalman filter
- Auto-alignment plus an interpolated flywheel lookup map, so shots stay accurate from anywhere on the field
- Swerve tuning on Kraken X60s, and current limits to prevent brownouts
- Autonomous routines worth about **50 points a match**
